🌊 Tsunami Safety Guide

Know what to do before, during and after a tsunami.

Tsunami Overview

A tsunami is a series of powerful ocean waves usually caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic activity or other disturbances.

🟡 Before Tsunami

  • Know if your area is at tsunami risk.
  • Identify evacuation routes.
  • Know the nearest safe shelter.
  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Learn local warning signals.

🔴 During Tsunami

  • Move inland or to higher ground.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Stay away from beaches.
  • Do not wait to watch the waves.
  • Stay alert for multiple waves.

🟢 After Tsunami

  • Return only after official permission.
  • Avoid damaged buildings.
  • Stay away from floodwater.
  • Listen to official updates.
  • Help vulnerable people safely.
SAFETY ACTIONS

Tsunami Do's & Don'ts

DO'S

  • Move to higher ground immediately.
  • Follow marked evacuation routes.
  • Carry your emergency kit.
  • Listen to official warnings.
  • Stay away from the coastline.
  • Help others evacuate safely.

DON'TS

  • Do not go to the beach to watch waves.
  • Do not wait for the wave to appear.
  • Do not return after the first wave.
  • Do not enter flooded coastal areas.
  • Do not ignore evacuation instructions.
  • Do not spread unverified information.
STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Tsunami Safety Guidelines

1 Know the Risk

Learn whether your area is located in a tsunami-risk zone.

2 Know the Route

Learn the nearest route to higher ground or a safe shelter.

3 Move Immediately

After a strong coastal earthquake or warning, move inland or to higher ground.

4 Stay Away

Stay away from beaches, rivers and coastal waterways.

5 Expect Multiple Waves

The first tsunami wave may not be the largest.

6 Wait for Clearance

Return only when authorities announce that it is safe.

Emergency

If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services.
