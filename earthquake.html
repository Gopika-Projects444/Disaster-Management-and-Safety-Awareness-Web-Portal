🌎 Earthquake Safety Guide

Learn earthquake preparedness and emergency safety measures.

Earthquake Overview

An earthquake is the sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement within the Earth's crust.

🟡 Before Earthquake

  • Secure heavy furniture.
  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Identify safe places.
  • Practice Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Keep emergency contacts available.

🔴 During Earthquake

  • Drop to the ground.
  • Take cover under a sturdy table.
  • Hold on until shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Move away from buildings if outside.

🟢 After Earthquake

  • Check for injuries.
  • Move away from damaged buildings.
  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Check for hazards.
  • Follow official instructions.
SAFETY ACTIONS

Earthquake Do's & Don'ts

DO'S

  • Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Use stairs during evacuation.
  • Check for injuries.
  • Follow official instructions.

DON'TS

  • Do not run during strong shaking.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Do not stand near windows.
  • Do not enter damaged buildings.
  • Do not touch damaged wires.
  • Do not spread false information.
STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Earthquake Safety Guidelines

1 Identify Safe Locations

Find safe locations inside your home, classroom or workplace.

2 Secure Objects

Secure heavy furniture and objects before an earthquake.

3 Drop, Cover and Hold

During shaking, drop down, take cover and hold on.

4 Check Injuries

After shaking stops, check yourself and others for injuries.

5 Expect Aftershocks

Be prepared for additional shaking after the main earthquake.

6 Follow Authorities

Follow official evacuation and safety instructions.

Emergency

Contact emergency services if you or others are in immediate danger.
