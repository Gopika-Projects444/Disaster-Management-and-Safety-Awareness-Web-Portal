⛰️ Landslide Safety Guide

Learn warning signs and safety measures for landslides.

Landslide Overview

A landslide is the movement of rock, soil or debris down a slope. Heavy rainfall, earthquakes and human activities can trigger landslides.

🟡 Before Landslide

  • Learn about landslide-prone areas.
  • Watch for cracks in slopes and walls.
  • Keep drainage systems clear.
  • Monitor heavy rainfall warnings.
  • Prepare an emergency kit.

🔴 During Landslide

  • Move away from the landslide path.
  • Move to safer ground.
  • Follow emergency instructions.
  • Avoid valleys and low areas.
  • Help vulnerable people safely.

🟢 After Landslide

  • Stay away from the affected area.
  • Watch for additional landslides.
  • Avoid damaged roads.
  • Report broken utility lines.
  • Return only after approval.
SAFETY ACTIONS

Landslide Do's & Don'ts

DO'S

  • Watch for cracks and warning signs.
  • Move away from unstable slopes.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Stay alert during heavy rainfall.
  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Report dangerous conditions.

DON'TS

  • Do not stay near unstable slopes.
  • Do not cross an active landslide area.
  • Do not ignore evacuation warnings.
  • Do not approach damaged buildings.
  • Do not return before authorities allow.
  • Do not travel through blocked roads.
STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Landslide Safety Guidelines

1 Identify Risk Areas

Learn whether your location is prone to landslides.

2 Monitor Rainfall

Pay attention to heavy rainfall and local warnings.

3 Watch Warning Signs

Look for cracks, unusual ground movement and changes in drainage.

4 Move Away

Move quickly away from unstable slopes and landslide paths.

5 Avoid Affected Areas

Do not enter areas affected by landslides.

6 Wait for Authorities

Return only after officials confirm that the area is safe.

Emergency

Contact emergency services if there is immediate danger.
