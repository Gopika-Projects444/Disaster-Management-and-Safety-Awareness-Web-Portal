🔥 Wildfire Safety Guide

Learn how to protect yourself from wildfire and smoke hazards.

Wildfire Overview

A wildfire is an uncontrolled fire that spreads through vegetation and can threaten forests, homes, wildlife and communities.

🟡 Before Wildfire

  • Keep dry vegetation away from buildings.
  • Prepare an evacuation kit.
  • Know multiple evacuation routes.
  • Follow local fire warnings.
  • Keep important documents ready.

🔴 During Wildfire

  • Evacuate when instructed.
  • Move away from the fire direction.
  • Close windows and doors.
  • Reduce smoke exposure.
  • Follow official updates.

🟢 After Wildfire

  • Return only after approval.
  • Watch for hot ash and hidden fires.
  • Avoid damaged power lines.
  • Check buildings for damage.
  • Continue monitoring warnings.
SAFETY ACTIONS

Wildfire Do's & Don'ts

DO'S

  • Follow evacuation orders immediately.
  • Carry your emergency kit.
  • Keep windows and doors closed when smoke is present.
  • Stay updated with official information.
  • Help vulnerable people evacuate.
  • Follow air-quality guidance.

DON'TS

  • Do not fight a large wildfire yourself.
  • Do not drive through heavy smoke.
  • Do not return to evacuated areas early.
  • Do not touch damaged electrical wires.
  • Do not ignore air-quality warnings.
  • Do not spread false information.
STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Wildfire Safety Guidelines

1 Prepare an Evacuation Plan

Plan multiple routes to reach a safe location.

2 Prepare Supplies

Keep water, food, medicines, flashlight and important documents ready.

3 Follow Warnings

Monitor official wildfire and evacuation warnings.

4 Evacuate Early

Leave immediately when authorities issue evacuation instructions.

5 Avoid Smoke

Reduce exposure to wildfire smoke and follow official health guidance.

6 Return Safely

Return only after officials declare the area safe.

Emergency

Contact emergency services when there is immediate danger.
