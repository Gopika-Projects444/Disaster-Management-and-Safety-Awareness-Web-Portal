🌪️ Cyclone Safety Guide

Stay prepared for strong winds, heavy rainfall and storm surges.

Cyclone Overview

A cyclone is a large rotating storm system that can produce strong winds, heavy rainfall, flooding and storm surges.

🟡 Before Cyclone

  • Monitor cyclone warnings.
  • Secure loose objects.
  • Store food and water.
  • Charge phones and power banks.
  • Identify the nearest shelter.

🔴 During Cyclone

  • Stay indoors.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Keep emergency supplies nearby.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Do not go outside during strong winds.

🟢 After Cyclone

  • Wait for official clearance.
  • Avoid fallen power lines.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings.
  • Check drinking water safety.
  • Report emergencies.
SAFETY ACTIONS

Cyclone Do's & Don'ts

DO'S

  • Stay updated through official warnings.
  • Secure doors and windows.
  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Move to a shelter if instructed.
  • Stay indoors during strong winds.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.

DON'TS

  • Do not stay near windows.
  • Do not go outside during the cyclone.
  • Do not touch fallen electrical wires.
  • Do not spread false warnings.
  • Do not return to evacuated areas early.
  • Do not travel through flooded roads.
STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Cyclone Safety Guidelines

1 Monitor Forecasts

Track official cyclone forecasts and warnings.

2 Secure Your Home

Secure loose objects, doors and windows before the storm.

3 Prepare Supplies

Keep food, water, medicines, flashlight and emergency items ready.

4 Evacuate

Move to a cyclone shelter if authorities issue an order.

5 Stay Indoors

Remain inside a strong building during dangerous winds.

6 Wait for Clearance

Go outside only after authorities declare it safe.

Emergency

Contact emergency services during an immediate emergency.
