🌊 Flood Safety Guide

Learn how to protect yourself before, during and after a flood.

Flood Overview

Floods occur when water overflows onto normally dry land. Heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, storms and poor drainage can cause flooding.

🟡 Before Flood

  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Store important documents safely.
  • Identify higher ground and shelters.
  • Keep phones and power banks charged.
  • Follow official flood warnings.

🔴 During Flood

  • Move to higher ground.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Avoid flooded roads and bridges.
  • Keep children away from floodwater.
  • Listen to official alerts.

🟢 After Flood

  • Return only when authorities allow.
  • Avoid contaminated water.
  • Check electrical systems.
  • Drink safe water.
  • Report damaged infrastructure.
SAFETY ACTIONS

Flood Do's & Don'ts

DO'S

  • Move to higher ground.
  • Keep an emergency kit ready.
  • Follow evacuation orders.
  • Keep emergency numbers available.
  • Use safe drinking water.
  • Stay updated through official alerts.

DON'TS

  • Do not walk through moving floodwater.
  • Do not drive through flooded roads.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment when wet.
  • Do not drink untreated floodwater.
  • Do not allow children to play in floodwater.
  • Do not ignore evacuation warnings.
STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Flood Safety Guidelines

Follow these important steps to reduce flood risk.

1 Monitor Warnings

Follow official flood and weather alerts.

2 Prepare Emergency Kit

Keep water, food, medicines, flashlight and important items ready.

3 Move Valuables

Keep documents and valuables in elevated and waterproof locations.

4 Evacuate

Move to a safe location when authorities issue an evacuation order.

5 Avoid Floodwater

Never walk or drive through dangerous floodwater.

6 Return Safely

Return home only after officials confirm that it is safe.
